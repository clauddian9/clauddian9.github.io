Privacy Policy

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Cloud. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, Hermes accesses the owner's own Google account data (such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets) only to carry out tasks the owner requests.

Use and sharing

This data is used solely to serve the owner. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored on the owner's private server. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
